# Content: show less, reveal more

Standing goal from the product owner: minimize text up front, keep screens easy to read and navigate
for the average student, and put heavy text in expandable or extractable sections.

Students open these screens under pressure. A wall of feedback after a hard rehearsal gets skimmed or
skipped; three clear points and a next step get acted on.

## Three levels

| Level | How students reach it | What goes here |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Glance | Always visible | Title, status, score, next action, errors, consent, deadlines |
| 2. Expand | One click, in place | Reasons, rubric, evidence, examples, rationale, full briefs |
| 3. Extract | Side panel, copy, or file | Transcripts, full feedback, case readings, exports |

## Budget per view

- Page title: 8 words or fewer.
- Visible summary: 2 sentences or fewer, about 40 words.
- Lists: 3 items visible, then "Show all (n)".
- One primary action.
- Disclosure summary lines name the content and its size: "Full brief · 4 min read".

## Where content goes

| Content | Level | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Four category scores with level words | 1 | Score cards |
| "One thing to practice next time" | 1 | Tinted panel under the scores |
| Overall summary | 1 | Two sentences in the Overview tab |
| Strength and growth-area titles, source and timecodes | 1 | Collapsed rows, 3 visible, "Show all" for more |
| Strength detail and evidence quote; growth detail and suggestion | 2 | Inside each row |
| Rubric notes, category notes | 2 | Content tab, one expandable row each |
| Delivery bands | 1 | Short label / word lists in the Delivery tab |
| Answer-by-answer delivery, camera observations | 2 | Expandable sections in the Delivery tab |
| Learning points (keep doing, work on next, rewatch) | 1 | Takeaways tab, one line each |
| Headline delivery and camera figures | 1 | Statistics tab, eight figures with one line each |
| Per-answer delivery and camera moments over time | 1 then 2 | Moments timeline; selecting a point opens its detail |
| Transcript excerpt for one answer | 2 | "What you said" in the timeline's detail panel |
| Full session transcript (if the page offers one) | 3 | Side panel with Copy and Download |
| Study-plan step rationale | 2 | Disclosure under each step |
| Case background reading | 3 | Side panel during setup; 3-bullet summary visible |
| Scenario instructions | 1 then 2 | 3 bullets visible, "Full brief" expands |
| Consent and recording details | 1 then 2 | One sentence and the consent control visible; policy expands |
| Errors | 1 | Inline, never collapsed |

## Rewriting heavy text: example

Before (report, all visible):
> Overall, you demonstrated a solid ability to structure your responses using the STAR framework,
> though there were several moments where your answers lacked specificity. In particular, when asked
> about leading through ambiguity, you described the situation in detail but spent comparatively
> little time on the actions you personally took... (300 more words)

After:
- Level 1: Content & Structure "2 / 5 · Developing". Practice next: "End each story with the result
  as a number." Summary: "Clear structure; your results need numbers."
  Rows: Strength "Framed the stakes early" (From the conversation). Growth area "Quantify the
  outcome" (1:42 · 4:10).
- Level 2: the growth-area row expands to two sentences of detail and one "Try this" suggestion.
- Level 3: if the page offers the transcript, "Full transcript · 18 turns" opens in a panel.

## Voice and microcopy

- Address the student as "you". Coaching voice is first person from the coach ("I noticed you…"),
  matching the existing study-plan voice.
- Name things the way students do: Practice, My Reports, Study Plans, Paths, Mastery. Not engine,
  route, or model names.
- Buttons are verbs that say what happens: "Start practice", "Build study plan", "End session".
- Errors say what happened and what to do: "Camera blocked. Allow camera access in your browser, then
  select Try again."
- No marketing copy, eyebrows, testimonials, or invented outcomes, stats, or quotes inside the app.
- Empty states say what will appear and how to make it appear: "Your reports appear here after your
  first practice. Start practice."
