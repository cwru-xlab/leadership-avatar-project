<non_negotiables>
**From CONTEXT.md — locked:**
1. **High-water mark, not current-slide.** The avatar's context contains every slide
   ever shown. Navigating back to 3 after reaching 7 does NOT un-show 4-7. Unshown
   slides never enter context. The cursor this config declares is therefore the
   HIGH-WATER MARK, and the server owns it (plan 14-05's ratchet). Nothing in this
   plan may read a "current slide".
2. **Deck scoring is all three:** text-derived structure and density; slide/speech
   correlation; and **visual review of the rendered slide images**. The third is why
   `buildEvaluationImages` exists — appearance must be judged on appearance, not on
   word counts.
3. **These are first-class rubric dimensions**, declared in `extraRubricDimensions`.
   13-CONTEXT.md explicitly rejected folding deck quality into `content` as a
   sub-point.
4. **Negotiation is BOTH.** The ask (price and equity) is a plain wizard form field,
   session-constant, in the system prompt from turn one — **not** an LLM extraction.
   The fair-value band is instance config: the avatar knows it, the student does not,
   and it is **never** part of the produced outcome record. Only the SETTLED terms
   come from the evaluator.
5. **Time envelope is SOFT.** 20-30 minutes is guidance. If it runs out mid-pitch the
   student may continue and the overrun is noted in the report. **No hard finish and
   no in-character meeting close that ends the session** — do not give this type an
   `avatarMayEnd` licence and do not write "wrap up, we're out of time, goodbye" into
   the prompt as a terminating move.
6. **Session length is a PROPOSAL** derived from slide count within the envelope, and
   the student can adjust it before starting. The clamp lives in
   `clampAdjustableBudget` (14-02); the proposal lives here.
7. **Prefix cache.** Slide reveal state is PER-TURN and must never enter the system
   prompt. This plan's `buildDeckSystemPrompt` must not take a cursor, a turn index
   or an elapsed time. The admitted slide text enters through the tail block, which
   plan 14-11 wires.
8. No Google Slides, no Drive, no OAuth.
</non_negotiables>

    want and then defend it with evidence; topical discussion should correlate with
