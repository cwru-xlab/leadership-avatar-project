<non_negotiables>
**From CONTEXT.md — locked:**
1. **Pitch subject is free text.** No `<select>`, no radio list of categories, no
   suggested-category chips, no autocomplete over a catalog. A textarea or input and
   nothing more. Placeholder examples in grey text are fine; a clickable list is not.
2. **Exactly three knowledge levels**, student-selected: completely blind / name and
   role only / full profile shown. This is a wizard choice. **It must not read or
   display Phase 8's `difficulty`** and must not be presented as a difficulty
   setting.
3. **Disclosure must match the level.** At `blind`, the step must NOT render the
   persona's role, interests or priorities — not in a tooltip, not in a collapsed
   panel, not in the DOM at all (a student reading view-source would defeat the whole
   exercise). At `name-role`, render the name and role ONLY. At `full-profile`,
   render the full persona. The page must therefore fetch only what the chosen level
   permits, or the chosen level must be known before the persona is fetched.
4. **Timer: visible by default, hideable MID-SESSION.** The collapse toggle lives in
   the session, not only in the wizard. Collapsing it must not pause or reset it.
5. **Soft, always.** When the window elapses the panel changes its own appearance and
   wording and nothing else. It must not disable the push-to-talk control, must not
   auto-submit the turn, must not close the mic, and must not emit any event the
   shell could act on. `PitchTimerPanel` takes no `onExpire` callback — by design.
6. **No engagement indicator.** No meter, no gauge, no bar, no dot, no colour-coded
   avatar border, no "interest" wording. The student reads the person. This was an
   explicit rejection.
</non_negotiables>

  panel and a single unobtrusive icon. **Collapsing must not stop the interval or
