<non_negotiables>
**From CONTEXT.md — locked:**
1. **Pitch subject is FREE TEXT.** No category list, no catalog, no enum. Product,
   startup or themselves all arrive as the same string and go into the
   session-constant prompt.
2. **Listener knowledge is three STUDENT-SELECTED levels** — `blind`, `name-role`,
   `full-profile`. It is a wizard choice. It is **NOT** derived from Phase 8's
   difficulty parameter and **NOT** a property of any scenario record. Do not read
   `difficulty` anywhere in this plan.
3. **Timing is a visible timer with a SOFT cutoff.** Past 60 seconds the avatar shows
   impatience and may interrupt in dialogue. **Nothing hard-stops the student's
   turn.** There is no turn cutoff, no forced mic close, no engine-side stop.
4. **Disengagement shows in DIALOGUE ONLY.** No engagement meter, no gauge, no
   numeric engagement value, no UI indicator, and no explicit verbal time warning
   beat ("I've got about a minute here") — the user rejected that specific line.
   The student must read the person.
5. **Early end is the model's own judgment, gated by a floor** — never engine-side
   behavioral thresholds. Do not add a "if replies get short, end it" rule anywhere.
6. **"Listener discovery & tailoring" is its OWN rubric dimension**, separate from
   concision and delivery, and it is scored at **all three** knowledge levels
   including `full-profile`.
7. **Follow-ups are engagement-scaled and open-ended** — no fixed count, no fixed
   duration. Do not set `targetQuestionCount`.
8. **REQ-73 / prefix cache.** The system prompt is SESSION-CONSTANT. The pitch
   subject, the knowledge level, the listener persona and the fair... (no fair band
   here) are all session-constant and belong there. The elapsed time, the
   over-window state and the turn count are per-turn and belong in the tail block via
   `buildTimeBudgetFragment`. If `assembleSystemPrompt`'s dev-mode guard can throw
   because of this plan, the plan is wrong.
</non_negotiables>

  <name>Task 1: The elevator live system prompt and evaluator prompt</name>
  <files>lib/pitch/elevator-prompts.ts</files>
  <action>
Read `lib/interview/prompts.ts` first for the house voice and the cache contract its
header documents, then write this module in the same style.

**`buildElevatorSystemPrompt(config: ResolvedSessionConfig): string`** — takes the
resolved config ONLY. File header must state: every input is session-constant; there
is no turn index, no timestamp, no elapsed seconds and no reveal state in this
function, because REQ-73 requires the prefix to be byte-stable across turns.

The prompt must contain:
- **Who the avatar is.** The full listener persona, always, in full, regardless of
  the student's chosen knowledge level. Add an explicit instruction: "The student may
  not know who you are. Do NOT volunteer your role, your priorities or your
  interests unprompted at the {blind} level; answer naturally if asked." Branch the
  *disclosure instruction* on `listenerKnowledge`, never the persona content —
  withholding is a student-UI concern plus a disclosure instruction, not a reason to
  hide information from the model that would make it incoherent.
  - `blind` — "The student knows nothing about you. Let them discover it."
  - `name-role` — "The student knows your name and role only. Your interests and
    priorities are yours to reveal if asked."
  - `full-profile` — "The student has read your profile. They should already know
    your priorities; notice if they ignore them."
- **What is being pitched:** `instance.pitchSubject`, verbatim, as free text, framed
  as "the student is about to pitch you the following, in their own words: ...".
- **The reward model, in the brief's own terms.** Verbatim in intent: the student
  should be rewarded for quickly getting to know you and finding common ground, then
  relating the pitch to your specific interests and perspectives — selling to YOU,
  not selling the thing in general. A pitch that is concise, gets the important
  information out and leaves room for follow-ups should earn engaged, probing
  follow-ups. A drawn-out, tedious, unrelatable pitch should make you less
  interested.
- **How disengagement shows: in dialogue only.** Instruct concretely — shorter and
  flatter replies, less elaboration, cutting to "so what's the ask?", closing the
  body of a reply rather than opening a new thread. Then forbid the alternatives
  explicitly in the prompt: do not announce how much time you have, do not say you
  are losing interest, do not narrate your own engagement level. (CONTEXT.md rejected
  both the meter and the verbal warning beat.)
- **The 60-second window, soft.** "A pitch like this should land in 30 to 60 seconds.
  You will be told in each turn how long the student has been talking. Past the
  window you may interrupt, redirect, or ask them to get to the point — in character.
  You never cut their microphone and you never refuse to listen." Note that the
  elapsed number itself arrives in the tail block, not here.
- **The licence to end the conversation, and its vocabulary.** "If the pitch is
  genuinely going nowhere you may end the conversation yourself, in character, by
  emitting {the termination marker 13-03 defined} with one of these reasons:
  `pitch_too_long`, `no_common_ground`, `unclear_ask`, `lost_interest`. End by
  excusing yourself as a real person would; do not explain that you are ending a
  practice session." Read `lib/engine/termination.ts`'s header for the EXACT marker
  syntax 13-03 chose and reproduce it — do not invent a second syntax.
- **No floor language in the prompt.** The floor is enforced in
  `resolveTermination` (14-02). Do not tell the model "you may not end before turn
  2" — a model asked to count its own turns will do it badly, and the server already
  guarantees it. Add a code comment saying so.

**`ELEVATOR_EVALUATOR_PROMPT`** — the grading system message. It must instruct
scoring of all six dimensions, naming the two extras explicitly:
- `discovery_tailoring` — "Did the student learn something specific about this
  listener before pitching, and did the pitch then speak to THAT person's stated
  interests and priorities? Score this at every knowledge level: when the student had
  the full profile, the bar is whether they USED it, not whether they asked for it."
  This sentence is what makes CONTEXT.md's "scored at all three knowledge levels
  including full profile" real.
- `concision` — "Did the pitch land the essential information inside roughly 30-60
  seconds, and did it leave room for follow-up rather than consuming the whole
  conversation?"
- Instruct that if the listener ended the conversation early, the grader must still
  score every dimension on what did happen, and must state the specific reasons and
  the approximate point at which interest dropped in the structured body. **Do not**
  instruct the grader to zero anything — the ceiling is applied in code
  (`lib/pitch/score-caps.ts`), not by the model.

**`buildElevatorEvaluationContext(config)`** — returns the per-type grading inputs:
pitch subject, listener knowledge level, the listener persona, the declared window
seconds, and the termination reason/timecode if present. Follow the shape 13-05's
`buildEvaluationContext` contract expects.
