<decision_recorded_here>
**The pitch-deck type gets Phase-13-style checkpointing.** This is the extension
14-RESEARCH.md Open Question 1 flagged. Phase 13 granted checkpointing to the four
interview presets and deliberately withheld it from `case-study` (13-07
non-negotiable 1) but said nothing about future types. The decision, made here and
not discovered later:

- `pitch-deck` → `checkpointing: "client-driven"`, same cadence trigger the
  interview types use. Reason: a 20-30 minute session carrying a server-authoritative
  slide cursor and a reveal trail needs a durable write; without a checkpoint, a
  browser crash at minute 25 loses the entire reveal history that Criterion 4's
  slide/speech correlation scoring is computed from.
- `pitch-elevator` → `checkpointing: "none"`. Reason: the session is a pitch plus
  follow-ups, minutes not tens of minutes, with no cursor to persist. Giving it a
  checkpoint would buy nothing and add a divergence to maintain.
- `case-study` → unchanged at `"none"`. Phase 13's divergence is preserved exactly.

**Where the high-water mark lives:** four narrow nullable columns on
`InteractionReport`, NOT inside `inputSnapshot`. `inputSnapshot` is session-constant
by contract (snapshotted at start, never re-fetched — 13-07's REQ-33 discipline);
the cursor is mutable by definition and must not be smuggled into a field whose
whole guarantee is immutability. Four nullable columns on an existing table is the
smallest additive change and matches 13-02's precedent of keeping genuinely
different pointers as separate columns rather than converging them into a tagged
blob.
</decision_recorded_here>

  `migration.sql` and read it; if it contains anything else, stop and report.

**Ratchet semantics (CONTEXT.md, locked):**
- Monotonic non-decreasing. Backward navigation NEVER lowers the mark —
  "navigating back to slide 3 after reaching slide 7 does not un-show 4-7."
- Forward jumps through the thumbnail strip DO advance it — "jumping forward reveals
  the skipped slides to the avatar as shown."
- Bounds-checked against the deck's real slide count from the instance. A client
  value above `slideCount - 1` is clamped, not accepted and not an error.
- The client value is never written through. The persisted value is always
  `max(stored ?? -1, min(requested, slideCount - 1))`.
</non_negotiables>
