<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Three-step guided builder component</name>
  <files>components/scenario/ScenarioBuilder.tsx</files>
  <action>
Create `components/scenario/ScenarioBuilder.tsx`, a client component implementing the
guided flow. Model its step machinery on `app/interview/[type]/page.tsx`'s `SetupStep`
union + conditional-render-per-step + progress indicator, so it speaks the visual
language students already learned there.

Props: `{ mode: "create" } | { mode: "edit"; caseId: string; initial: CaseStudy }`.

State: `type BuilderStep = "situation" | "characters" | "criteria" | "review"` plus the
draft (`name`, `backgroundInfo`, `avatars: CaseAvatar[]`, `evaluationPrompt`). In edit
mode, seed the draft from `initial` and start on `"situation"`.

Render a step header/progress strip showing "Step N of 4" and the four step names, with
completed steps marked — one step's fields visible at a time, never all of them.

**Step 1 — Situation.** An `Input` for the scenario name and a `Textarea` for the
background/situation, with helper copy explaining that this is what every character in
the roleplay knows. Show a live character count against
`SCENARIO_LIMITS.backgroundMin` from `lib/scenario/validation.ts` — import the shared
limits, never hardcode duplicate numbers. "Next" is disabled until both pass.

**Step 2 — Characters.** A list of character cards, each with:
`<AvatarPickerGrid value={avatar.profileId ?? null} onChange={...}>`, an `Input` for the
character's name, an `Input` for their role, and a `Textarea` for
`additionalInfo` (labelled as the private briefing only this character knows — say
plainly that the student will not see it during the run). Mint each character's `id`
client-side with `crypto.randomUUID()`. "Add another character" appends; a remove control
is disabled when only one remains. No cap on the count. "Next" is disabled until at least
one character has an avatar, a name and a role.

**Step 3 — Criteria.** A `Textarea` for `evaluationPrompt`, framed with copy that makes
the composition rule honest: explain that every run is already graded on conversational
adequacy and emotional intelligence by a standard rubric, and that what they write here
is applied IN ADDITION to it. Do NOT imply they can replace the rubric. Do NOT promise
visual or vocal scoring — Phase 10 owns that and this phase renders those "Not yet
measured". "Next" disabled until the minimum length passes.

**Step 4 — Review & Save.** A read-only summary of all three steps. A single primary
"Save scenario" button. On press:
- Run `validateScenarioInput` from `lib/scenario/validation.ts` client-side first and, on
  failure, jump back to the step owning the first failing field with the message inline.
- POST `{name, backgroundInfo, evaluationPrompt, avatars}` to `/api/scenario/add`
  (create) or `{id, ...}` to `/api/scenario/edit` (edit), `credentials: "include"`.
  NEVER include `ownerId`, `published`, `cohortIds`, `createdBy`.
- On a 400 with field errors, surface them inline on their owning step rather than a
  generic toast.
- On success: `addToast` confirming the save, then `router.push("/case-play")`. This is
  REQ-28: saving is its own step and ends on the list, where the new scenario is
  immediately startable — do NOT navigate into `/case-play/[caseId]` and do NOT auto-start
  a session.
- Guard against double-submit with an `isSaving` flag on the button.

Add a "Cancel" / back-to-list control that returns to `/case-play` without saving, with a
confirm prompt if the draft has any content.
