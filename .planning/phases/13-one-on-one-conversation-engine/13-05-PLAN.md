Collapse the two evaluators and two evaluation runners into one of each, and make
the evaluator's JSON schema derive from the type's rubric dimensions instead of
hardcoding four.

four shared plus extras), REQ-72 (Visual and Vocal structurally un-skippable).

to `lib/report/structured.ts`, and new assertions in the existing
`scripts/verify-report-structure.ts`.

The old per-type evaluator modules are NOT deleted here — their callers still
exist. Plan 13-13 deletes them once no route imports them.
</objective>
