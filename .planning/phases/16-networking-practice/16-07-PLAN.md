**1. `NETWORKING_RUBRIC_EXTRAS: RubricDimension[]`** — exactly three, each with a one-
or-two-sentence `description` stating what a 1 and a 5 look like:
- `rapport` / "Rapport" — did a real two-way connection form. 1: a one-sided
  transaction, the other person stayed a stranger. 5: genuine mutual interest, the other
  person volunteered things they were not asked.
- `self-introduction` / "Self-Introduction" — was who the student is, and what they do,
  clear and concise. 1: rambling or so vague the listener could not place them. 5: placed
  themselves in a sentence or two and gave the listener something to hook onto.
- `goal-progress` / "Goal Progress" — how far the student got toward what they actually
  wanted. 1: never steered the conversation there at all. 5: made the ask clearly, at a
  moment the conversation had earned.
  Doc comment on this one: "The avatar never saw the goal. This dimension grades the
  student's steering, not the avatar's cooperation — a declined ask that was well made
  can still score high, and a 5 on Rapport beside a 1 here is exactly the feedback this
  type exists to give (16-CONTEXT.md Specific Ideas)."

**2. `buildNetworkingSystemPrompt(input)`** — the live avatar prompt.
- `input` is a NARROW, explicit parameter object: `{ persona: string; displayName: string }`
  and nothing else. **It must not accept the goal, the rubric or the outcome shape.**
  Add a doc comment: "This signature is deliberately narrow. The student's goal is
  withheld from the avatar (16-CONTEXT.md) and the way that is guaranteed is that this
  function cannot receive it. Do not widen this parameter to the resolved config."
- Assemble from an ORDERED ARRAY of clause strings, joined with blank lines, so a clause
  can be inserted later. Comment above the array: "A `setting` clause (conference
  reception, coffee chat, …) is DEFERRED per 16-CONTEXT.md's Deferred Ideas. It would be
  inserted here, between the persona clause and the manner clause, without restructuring
  anything. Do not add it now."
- Clauses, in the plain-prose register the existing distill prompt uses (no markdown):
  1. `You are playing the role of: ${persona}` — the same slot-in contract both a
     character record and a distilled persona satisfy.
  2. You are meeting this person for the first time in a professional context. You do not
     know them and have no prior relationship.
  3. Behave like a real person being approached by a stranger: you are willing to talk,
     but you have your own time, your own interests and your own reasons to engage or
     disengage. You are not an interviewer and you must not interview them — do not work
     through a list of questions.
  4. Speak in short conversational turns. Ask about them when you are genuinely curious,
     and say so when you are not. Volunteer something about yourself when the
     conversation earns it.
  5. Do not coach, do not evaluate, do not narrate, and never break character to comment
     on the conversation.
  6. **Walk-away clause**: if the conversation becomes genuinely unrewarding — the other
     person is not engaging you, is only talking about themselves, or is making you
     uncomfortable — you may end the conversation politely, the way a real person would.
     Emit the termination marker exactly as the engine's protocol requires (use whatever
     marker contract `lib/engine/termination.ts` / 13-03 established — read it; do not
     invent a marker) with one of the declared reasons. Add a comment: the engine's floor
     rejects an end that lands too early, and a rejected end means the conversation
     simply continues with no student-visible error.
- **No clause mentioning a goal, an objective, an ask or what the student wants.**

**3. `NETWORKING_EVALUATOR_PROMPT`** — the evaluator's type-specific prompt text. It must:

  quality in the interview sense is explicitly not what is being judged (P16-SC4);
- describe the three extra dimensions in scoring terms (reuse the `NETWORKING_RUBRIC_EXTRAS`
  descriptions rather than restating them differently — import and interpolate);
- state that the student had a GOAL the avatar never saw, quote-reference that the goal
  is supplied in the evaluation context, and instruct the evaluator to grade Goal
  Progress on the student's steering toward it;
- instruct production of the outcome record's fields (below), stating explicitly that
  `"never-asked"` is a legitimate and common outcome and must be reported honestly
  rather than softened;
- if the session ended early on the avatar's initiative, instruct the evaluator to grade
  all seven dimensions anyway and to treat the early end as evidence, not as a missing
  session. Follow whatever early-end convention Phase 14's `14-04`/`14-14` established
  if it has landed — read it; otherwise record the choice in the SUMMARY.

**4. `buildNetworkingEvaluationContext(resolved, snapshot)`** — returns the object the
one evaluator sends as its user message. It MUST include: the persona/display name, the
character id or persona id, and **the student's goal text**. Doc comment: "This is the
one and only place the goal is admitted. The live prompt builder cannot receive it; the
evaluator must. That asymmetry IS the feature."
Match whatever shape `13-05`'s `runEvaluation` expects — read the real signature.

**5. The outcome field declaration** — export the `OutcomeRecordConfig` value (or the
field list it takes) for:
- `askMade: boolean`
- `askOutcome: "agreed" | "deflected" | "declined" | "never-asked"`
- `commonGround: string | null`
Doc comment: 16-CONTEXT.md decision 12 — "concrete, pointable feedback distinct from the
