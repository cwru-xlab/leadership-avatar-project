  Do not fabricate filler.

  the other person ended the conversation and give the recorded reason in plain language
  (map the `avatarEndReasons` vocabulary from 16-07 to student-facing phrasing — do not
  show the raw enum). Neutral styling. A short line that all seven dimensions are still
  scored from what happened.
- Missing-outcome handling: if `outcome` is null (an older row, or an evaluation that
  produced no outcome), render NOTHING rather than an empty scaffold. The panel is
  additive.
