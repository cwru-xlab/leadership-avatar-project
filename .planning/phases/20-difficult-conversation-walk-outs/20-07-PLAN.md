**Run A — the false-positive guard (P20-SC2, the one that matters most).**
1. Start `Firing a team member` (seeded) at the default `guarded` difficulty.
2. Play it the way the scenario requires: firm, clear, uncomfortable. Say, in
   your own words and at least once verbatim: *"Your performance has been
   unacceptable and this is your final warning."* Also use: "This decision is
   final", "I am ending your employment", "HR has signed off", "I will escalate
   this if we cannot agree a plan."
3. **Expected: the session does NOT end on you.** No walk-out, no final
   statement, no auto-finish. Play at least eight assistant turns.
4. If it ends: that is a FAIL on P20-SC2 and the phase does not ship. Record
   the exact line that tipped it.

**Run B — hostility accumulates and ends it in character (P20-SC1, P20-SC4).**
1. Start `Confronting a low-performing teammate`.
2. Over successive turns, attack the PERSON, not the work: name-calling,
   contempt, "you're useless", ALL-CAPS shouting, a threat to their standing.
3. Expected: nothing ends on the first sharp remark. Pressure accumulates, and
   after the four-assistant-turn floor the character ends the conversation **in
   character** — a person leaving a conversation, with no mention of a rule, a
   policy, a moderator, a report or the app.
4. During the final line, try typed Send and push-to-talk. Both must be locked.
5. While locked, confirm the **support note is still visible** and the **End
   session control is still present and still clickable**. This is Phase 15's
   guarantee surviving an avatar-initiated ending.
6. Expected: the session auto-finishes without you pressing End session, with no
   long dead pause after the avatar stops speaking (Phase 18's REQ-82 fix).
7. Now try to lower it: apologize sincerely and be cooperative for two turns
   BEFORE the floor is reached, then check the session still ends. The value
   must not recover (REQ-99).

**Run C — the severe carve-out below the floor (P20-SC3).**
1. Start any seeded conversation. On the FIRST or SECOND student turn — before
   four assistant turns have happened — say something in the severe category.
2. Expected: the session ends anyway, below the floor, and **still in
   character**. No break-frame message, no moderator voice.
3. Open the report and confirm the recorded termination reason is the severe
   one (`offensive_content`), distinct from Run B's `escalated`.

**Run D — the report (P20-SC5).**
1. Open the reports from Runs B and C.
2. Confirm: normal early-end chrome, not a FAILED-evaluation error; the
   decline panel shows observable causes at `m:ss` session-clock times; the
   in-role reaction's causes carry timecodes.
3. **Confirm the Run C report does NOT recite the explicit remark.** It must be
   characterized (e.g. "a slur directed at the character — not repeated here")
   while the timecode and the effect are intact.
4. Confirm Run B's report DOES show the hostile language verbatim — the student
   must be able to see what they actually said.
5. Confirm no rubric dimension is zeroed or capped: all eight dimensions carry
