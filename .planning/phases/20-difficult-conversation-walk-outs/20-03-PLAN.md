<objective>
Put the walk-out threshold on the `difficult-conversation` TYPE record — one
number, inherited by all seven seeded conversations and every authored scenario
— and guard mechanically that no per-scenario threshold surface can be added
later.

safety-adjacent number, and that there is no new validation surface to get
wrong. The absence of a per-scenario field is the requirement, which means it
has to be enforced by a guard in Phase 16's spirit rather than by a reviewer
remembering.

`disengagementWeights` profile, a new severe-category member of
`avatarEndReasons`, and an `avatarEndReasonByCause` mapping; and
`scripts/verify-dc-surface-count.ts` gains an eleventh section that fails if a
per-scenario threshold appears anywhere.
</objective>
