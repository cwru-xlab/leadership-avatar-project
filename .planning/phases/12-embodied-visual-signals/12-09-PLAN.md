**Session A** (camera-on, deliberate arm-waving, progressive slumping, face

**Session B** (student deliberately far to the side, body mostly out of frame).

Environment scored 1/5 and said so plainly. Body language nonetheless reported:
  - Gesturing: **Very still**
  - Hands near face: **Frequent**
  - Posture drift: **Held steady from the opening posture**
  - Measured from: **Shoulder line and Head position**

That is sign-off item 7, failed.

**Root cause, two independent defects:**

1. `lib/metrics/visual-capture.ts:2328` — `postureSignalsMeasured` filters on
   `poseVisibleSamples[signal] >= POSTURE_BASELINE_MIN_SAMPLES`, which is `15`
   (`body-thresholds.ts:135`). That is an ABSOLUTE floor with no proportional
   component. In a ~165s session, 15 pose samples is roughly 6% visibility — a
   few seconds of leaning into frame clears it, and the signal is then declared
   measured for the entire session. `POSTURE_BASELINE_MIN_SAMPLES`'s own comment
   says it is an achievability fix, not a behavioural cutoff — it was never
   intended to carry this gate.

2. `lib/metrics/bands.ts:380` — `bandPostureDrift` is binary (trip vs "Held
   steady") with NO unreadable branch, and `visualBodyLanguageBands` renders it
   whenever `posture_drift_mean` is a number, entirely independent of
   `posture_signals_measured`. Even with defect 1 fixed, an empty signals array
   would still print "Held steady".

The honest wording already exists and never fired: `describeMeasuredFrom` returns
`"Nothing — body not visible in frame"` for the empty case (`bands.ts:334`).

**Secondary observations from the same sign-off** (lower confidence, addressed in
Task 2, NOT blockers for item 7):
  - Posture drift read "Held steady" in Session A despite deliberate progressive
    slumping, and in Session B where it was provably wrong. `12-TUNING.md`
    already calls `POSTURE_DRIFT_TRIP` "the weakest-evidenced cutoff in this
    file" with its true-positive side unverified. Two sessions, two "Held
    steady", one demonstrably false. Treat the true-positive side as unproven.
  - Phone reported "about 2 seconds" against a sustained in-frame phone.
    `PHONE_SCORE_THRESHOLD` (0.5) was NOT re-tuned in 12-08 because no dump ever
    captured the object model's raw per-detection confidences.
  - "Excessive gesturing at times" rendered with a "Try this:" and NO question,
    while "Frequent hand-to-face movement" correctly asked one. The
    describe-then-ask validator appears not to cover that entry. (The firing
    itself looks correct — it matches the real 1:26 arm-waving, so the 12-08
    `GESTURE_WINDOW_EXCESSIVE_PCT` fix is working.)
</observed_failure>

<constraint_do_not_overcorrect>
**REQ-51 pulls in the opposite direction and must not be broken by this fix:**

> "A partially visible body is scored on the landmarks that ARE available rather
> than skipped, and every posture comment states which were measured."

So the fix is NOT "tighten the floor until marginal sessions are skipped". A
student half in frame must still be scored on the landmarks that are genuinely
available, with the "Measured from" row naming them. The gate must separate:

  - **partial** — this signal was usably visible for a meaningful share of the
    session → score it, name it in "Measured from" (REQ-51's rule, unchanged)
  - **absent** — this signal was essentially never usably visible → report it as
    unreadable, score nothing, credit nothing (the item-7 fix)

A proportional floor distinguishes these; the current absolute floor of 15
cannot. Getting this boundary wrong in either direction is a failure: too loose
and item 7 fails again, too tight and REQ-51 fails instead.

Per `12-CONTEXT.md`'s calibration rule, when the two sides are in tension the
unreadable side wins — a false "held steady" credits a student for something
never observed, which is worse than declining to score a marginal body.
</constraint_do_not_overcorrect>
