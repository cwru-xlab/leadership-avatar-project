Recorded 2026-10-03 at 12-10's Task 4, run by the user.

Items 1 and 2 PASSED — the frame-bounds gating works and REQ-53 is met. **Item 3
FAILED as a finding.** The user ran a fully-in-frame session and "slumped a lot".
The report said:

- Framing: **Well centred** · On camera: **Present throughout** · Steadiness: Steady
- Narrative: "Camera centering was strong (83% of the time) and your face was
  always in frame..."
- BODY LANGUAGE: Posture drift **"Held steady from the opening posture"**,
  Measured from **"Shoulder line and Head position"**

The user's own observation: *"because of the camera framing, it struggles to
track this."*

**The new gating is NOT the cause.** Both signals cleared the 0.60 in-frame
cutoff; this was a genuine, well-framed session and the producer correctly
declared both signals measured. The failure is downstream, in what those signals
can represent and how they are aggregated.

**Three structural causes, all verified in code at 12-10's close, none measured:**

1. **The mean dilutes across signals.** `computePostureDrift`
   (`visual-capture.ts:556-582`) returns the arithmetic mean of per-signal
   normalized deltas. A slump is forward-head-dominant; shoulder-line tilt is
   roughly orthogonal to it. A large delta averaged with a near-zero one lands
   at half before `POSTURE_DRIFT_TRIP` (0.5) is consulted.

2. **It dilutes AGAIN across the session.** `posture_drift_mean`
   (`visual-capture.ts:2651`) is the session-wide mean of every post-baseline
   tick's already-averaged drift, and `bandPostureDrift` (`bands.ts:409`)
   compares that against the same 0.5. So it must clear the trip after averaging
   across signals AND across a session that opens upright by design (the first
   `POSTURE_BASELINE_WINDOW_S` = 20s establishes the baseline).
   `posture_drift_max_s` — the sustained-streak metric that would actually
   catch a slump — is computed (`:1711`) and persisted but **the band never
   reads it**.

3. **`forwardHeadOffset` may not measure forward head at all.** It is
   `Math.hypot(dx, dy) / shoulderWidth` (`worker.ts:614`) — a 2D distance from
   the shoulder midpoint to the nose, not anterior displacement. True
   forward-head translation runs along the camera axis and is near-invisible to
   a frontal webcam. What a slump actually registers is the head dropping
   *toward* the shoulder line, which **reduces** that distance. The label is a
   misnomer for the measured quantity, and the sign may be backwards relative to
   the behaviour being graded.

   Meanwhile `shoulderTiltDeg` is `atan2(|dy|, |dx|)` in degrees
   (`worker.ts:597`) — the shoulder line's angle to horizontal, observed at
   4.9-6.3 degrees upright. A vertical slump barely moves it.

Cause 3 is the one that decides this plan's shape. Causes 1 and 2 are fixable by
changing aggregation. If cause 3 holds, no aggregation change rescues the signal,
because neither channel encodes the behaviour.
</observed_failure>
