<objective>
Surface the new contract in the report and in both evaluator prompts: a "Body
language" subheading for the scored signals, a separate unscored Observations
section, a kind-tagged single Moments timeline, and prompt language that forbids
scoring or crediting anything descriptive.

Purpose: this phase exists because a session spent waving both arms produced a
report that said nothing about it — and because an earlier session with several
people gesturing obscenely was *commended* for "minimal obvious fidgeting or
posture concerns". Adding flags without fixing how they are presented would
reintroduce the second defect in inverted form, with a student reading an
unscored observation as a penalty. The presentation and the prompt contract are
therefore part of the capability, not a follow-up.
Output: the rendered sections, the tagged timeline, and both evaluator contracts.
</objective>

   because no sensor here measured a viewer's attention. This mirrors the
   existing RULE ON INTERNAL STATES, so place it adjacent to that rule.

5. Add the posture rule: the SCORE comes from `posture_drift_*`, which is drift
   against the student's OWN opening posture, never against an upright ideal.
   The absolute readings in `observations` are NOT a grade. Every posture comment
   must state which signals were available, naming
   `posture_signals_measured` in plain words — unconditionally, unlike the
   existing coverage disclosure which only surfaces when coverage is poor. When
   `posture_signals_measured` is empty, say the body was not visible enough to
   read posture; do not fall silent, and do not substitute `posture_flags`.

6. Add a HARD RULE ON observations, written as strongly as the existing HARD RULE

   but NEVER SCORED. It may be described factually and must not influence the
   visual score, be cited as a strength or a growth area, or be turned into an
   inference. Specifically: "a phone was visible for about 40 seconds" is
   allowed; "you were distracted" or "you were checking your phone" is forbidden

   never be graded, because it overlaps heavily with stimming, ADHD and anxiety

8. Keep the existing HARD RULE ON not_measured intact and add one clause: the
