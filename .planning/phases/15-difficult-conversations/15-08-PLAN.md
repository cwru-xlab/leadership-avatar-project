Ship the live surface: the briefing and difficulty steps, the out-of-band End-session
control and support note, and the student's side of the recognize-offer-confirm close.

Purpose: two locked decisions make this plan's deliverables **required, not polish**.

*therefore* obliges the session screen to carry non-avatar affordances: a visible

must read the person" — the difficulty is chosen here and then hidden entirely, with no
meter and no indicator, exactly as Phase 14 rejected its engagement meter.

including the real-distress case.
</objective>

<non_negotiables>
**From CONTEXT.md — locked:**
1. **Full briefing, hidden position withheld.** The student reads situation, roles,
   shared backstory and their own objective before starting. **Do not** build a
   student-selected briefing level — Phase 14's elevator pattern is deliberately NOT
   reused here. **Do not** build a situation-only cold open. Both rejected.
2. **Difficulty is the ONLY dial.** One step, three bands. No industry reframing, no role
   reframing, no second parameter of any kind.
3. **Difficulty is hidden entirely during the session.** No indicator, no meter, no
   gauge, no icon, no colour change, no label. Nothing on the live screen reveals the
   band or the character's state.
4. **The End-session control is ALWAYS visible and ALWAYS available** — from the first
   second, not after a minimum, not inside a menu, not only when the avatar is idle. It
   is the affordance that carries the real-distress case, and it only works if it is
   there before the student needs it.
5. **The support note is static app copy.** It is never generated, never spoken by the
   avatar, and never rendered inside the conversation transcript. It must be visually
   obviously the app talking, not the character.
6. **The avatar never breaks character.** Nothing in this plan may inject an
   out-of-character avatar line, replace an avatar turn, or trigger a system message into
   the transcript. If you find yourself adding one, you are re-implementing the rejected
   safety-exception variant.
7. **Recognize → offer → confirm.** The confirm is a student action. The session never
   ends on the avatar's inference alone. One extra click, by design.
8. **An avatar walk-out is an OUTCOME, not an error.** No error toast, no crash state, no
   "session failed" copy. It transitions to the report like any other finish.
</non_negotiables>
