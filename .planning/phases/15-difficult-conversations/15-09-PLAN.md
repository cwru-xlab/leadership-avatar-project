<objective>
Ship the report side: eight dimensions, a factual outcome record that is visibly not a
score, a named banner for an avatar-ended conversation, and the character's private
reaction with the turns that caused it.

Purpose: Success Criterion 4 — *the report judges how the conversation was handled,
clarity, empathy, holding the line, not merely that the student reached the end of it.*
The structural risk is the pairing CONTEXT.md locks: a scored "Objective achieved" that
judges the APPROACH sitting beside an unscored factual outcome record. If the page blends
them, the criterion is lost on the page even when the evaluator got it right. This plan
renders them as two different kinds of thing and proves it.
Output: DTO surfacing, three panels through the extras slot, a verification script, and a
human read of a real report.
</objective>

<non_negotiables>
**From CONTEXT.md — locked:**
1. **The outcome record is NEVER scored and never affects a score.** It does not cap, lift
   or weight any dimension. Phase 14's early-end cap pattern is explicitly **not** reused.
   `postProcessScores` is deliberately absent from the type (15-06) — do not add it here
   and do not apply any adjustment in the DTO or in a panel.
2. **"Objective achieved" scores the APPROACH, not the result.** The page must not imply
   otherwise: no check/cross iconography on that card, no "goal met" styling tied to it,
   no derived "success" badge computed from the two together.
3. **Avatar-ended conversations use Phase 14's report pattern exactly:** a named outcome
   banner with the specific reasons AND the timecode where it turned, and **every rubric
   dimension still scored on what did happen.** No zeroing, no error-shaped report.
4. **The in-role reaction is report-side only.** It is the character's private reaction
   plus the specific turns that caused it. It is not evaluator coaching voice — rejected.
   Nothing about it touches the live session.
5. **No slug branch in the report page.** The panels arrive through the extras slot. This
   is the one thing every Phase 13 and 14 plan forbids.
6. **No `hiddenPosition` on the report.** It is absent from the snapshot by construction
   (15-01); assert it is also absent from every rendered panel.
</non_negotiables>
