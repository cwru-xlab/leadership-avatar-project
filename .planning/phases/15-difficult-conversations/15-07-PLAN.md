<non_negotiables>
**From CONTEXT.md — locked:**
1. **Three separate sections, seeded first:** "Featured conversations" (seeded), then
   the student's own, then "From other students". **Not** one mixed list with
   per-card author attribution — that was rejected. Provenance is obvious from the
   section the card is in.
2. **Authored scenarios are structurally identical to seeded ones.** The form asks for
   the same six fields: the avatar's role, the situation, the shared backstory, the
   character's hidden position, the student's objective, the stakes (plus a title, the
   student's own role, the default difficulty and the avatar pick). **No free-text prose
   plus AI distillation** and **no minimal role-plus-situation form** — both rejected.
3. **Rejection = reason plus what to change.** Never a generic refusal. Show the
   category, the reason and the fix, and state that the scenario is still saved and
   still privately playable.
4. **`rejected` (422) and `unavailable` (503) must read differently.** The second must
   not accuse the author.
5. **No appeal control, no report button, no flag, no moderation UI, no admin view.**
   Phase 11 removed staff roles. If a student disagrees with the verdict, their remedy is
   editing.
6. **Edits are live.** No "republish to apply" affordance, no draft-versus-live toggle,
   no frozen published copy — rejected. Saving a published scenario changes what other
   students see (after the re-check).
7. **The hidden position is shown ONLY to the record's owner, in the builder.** It must
   not appear on any card, in any section, or on any read of someone else's scenario.
8. **No difficulty indicator leaks toward the session.** The card may show the record's
   default band as metadata; the live session shows nothing (plan 15-08).
</non_negotiables>

**`ConversationBuilder.tsx`** — one form, used by both pages, taking an optional existing
record. Fields in the order a student would think about them, each with a short helper
line explaining what it is FOR:
1. **Title** — "What do you call this situation?"
2. **Who you are** (`studentRole`) and **who they are** (`avatarRole`).
3. **The situation** — shown to whoever plays it.
4. **What you both already know** (`sharedBackstory`) — "Facts both sides have. The
   player sees this before they start."
5. **What they privately think** (`hiddenPosition`) — helper: "The player never sees
   this. It is what the character believes, wants and will not volunteer: their excuse,
   their counter-argument, their bottom line. This is what makes the conversation hard."
   Mark it visually as the hidden field so the author cannot mistake it for briefing text.
6. **The player's goal** (`studentObjective`) and **what is at stake** (`stakes`).
7. **Starting difficulty** — the three bands with their one-line meanings, as the
   record's default.
8. **The character's avatar** — `AvatarPickerGrid`.

Validation: run `validateDifficultConversationInput` **on the client**, from the same
module the server uses, and show every failing field inline at once with the validator's
own reason-plus-fix messages. Never show a client-only message that the server would
word differently.

Save → `POST /api/difficult-conversation/add` (or `/edit`). Render all server-returned
field errors if the two ever disagree.

**The publish control.** A single toggle with explicit copy about what publishing means:
"Anyone can find and play it. You stay its owner and can change or unpublish it at any
time." On submit:
- **200** → publish state updates in place with a confirmation line.
- **422** → render a blocked panel: the category in plain words ("This can't be published
  yet"), the `reason`, the `fix` as the prominent next step, and the locked reassurance
  — *"Your scenario is saved, and you can still practise it yourself right now."* Offer
  exactly two actions: edit the text, or practise it privately. **No appeal, no
  "request review", no contact link.**
- **503** → render a clearly different panel: "We couldn't run the safety check just
  now — nothing is wrong with your scenario. Try publishing again in a minute." With a
  Retry action. This panel must not contain the words rejected, blocked, or violation.
- On an **edit** that came back 422/503 with `demoted: true`, state both facts in order:
  "Your changes were saved. This scenario has been unpublished until the problem below is
  fixed."

**`app/conversations/new/page.tsx`** — the builder in create mode. On a successful save,
offer two clear next steps side by side: **Practise it now** (links straight to
`/practice/difficult-conversation/{newId}` — this is P15-SC2's "practise it
immediately") and **Publish it**. Do not auto-publish and do not make publishing the
default path.

**`app/conversations/[id]/page.tsx`** — owner-only edit. Server-side, load through the
owner-scoped loader; a non-owner or an unknown id renders the app's standard not-found
page (never a 403 and never "you don't own this"). A seeded id also renders not-found —
seeded records have no owner (15-04) and are edited by code review.
