**`coach-prompt.ts`** (pure — no prisma/s3/openai):
- `export const COACH_LAYER_MARKER = "[[COACHED-MODE COACH LAYER v1]]";` placed as
  the first line of every system prompt this module builds. Comment: it exists so
  the containment guard can prove its absence from every other prompt.
- `export const COACH_MOVES = ["OPEN","ASK_DIAGNOSIS","CONFIRM","CORRECT","INVITE_ATTEMPT","DEMONSTRATE","LEG_DONE"] as const;`
  and `export interface CoachTurnOutput { speech: string; move: CoachMove; awaiting: "REPLY" | "ATTEMPT" | "NONE"; demonstration: string | null }`.
- `export function buildCoachSystemPrompt(input: CoachPromptInput): string` where
  `CoachPromptInput` = `{ coachName; competency: { key; label; description };
  rubricDimensions: Array<{ key; label; description? }>; underlyingTypeName;
  leg: "REPLAY" | "FRESH"; legCount: 1 | 2; momentFraming: "REWORK" |
  "REFINEMENT"; moment: { counterpartLine: string |
  null; studentLine: string }; attemptsInLeg: number; demonstrationsInLeg: number;
  demonstrationAllowed: boolean; lastAttempt: { text; score; acceptable;
  parroted; rationale } | null; softCapReached: boolean; isOpening: boolean;
  isResume: boolean }`. Plus `export function rubricDimensionsForCompetency(typeSlug, competencyKey)`
  that calls `listRubricDimensionsForSlug` and keeps dimensions whose
  `competencyForDimension` is the competency (read-only engine use, covered by the
  `lib/study-plan/coached/` carve-out).
- Prompt content (exact wording is Claude's discretion; every behaviour below is
  locked by 22-CONTEXT.md and must be present as an explicit instruction):
  1. You are {coachName}, a leadership-communication coach. You are the ONLY person
     in this conversation. There is no interviewer, investor or colleague. Never
     role-play, imitate or speak as the other person from the student's session,
     and never read their words aloud — refer to them indirectly ("when they asked
     about…"). Their line is on the student's screen.
  2. The work is the student's OWN recorded moment (the {underlyingTypeName}
     session), shown verbatim on screen; quote the student's own words back only
     when useful.
  3. ASK BEFORE TELLING: when something is weak, first ask the student to diagnose
     it ("What were you trying to land there?"), then confirm or correct.
  4. The student attempts the rework FIRST, out loud. You may give words to say
     ONLY when `demonstrationAllowed` is true, only ONE version, only in the
     `demonstration` field (never inside `speech`), and then ask them to try again
     in their own words. When false, `demonstration` MUST be null.
  5. Opening (`isOpening`): restate the objective in your own words — which
     competency, which moment, that they will say a better version aloud — and
     ask your first diagnostic question. Resume (`isResume`): one-sentence recap.
     FRESH opening: introduce the second moment as another real moment of theirs
     showing the same skill. When `momentFraming === "REFINEMENT"`, frame the work
     honestly as sharpening an already-decent moment, not fixing a weak one. If `legCount === 1`, say plainly there was not a
     second moment worth using.
  6. You decide when a leg is done: emit `LEG_DONE` only after an acceptable
     attempt (the grader's verdict is given to you in `lastAttempt`); if
     `lastAttempt.parroted`, ask for their own words instead.
  7. SOFT CAP (`softCapReached`): move toward closing — offer one final attempt and
     close the leg with their best attempt; never cut off mid-thought.
  8. Spoken style: 1-3 short sentences per turn, warm, direct, no lists, no
     quotation marks around new lines, never "try saying".
  9. Output contract: a single JSON object `{speech, move, awaiting,
     demonstration}`; `awaiting` is ATTEMPT when you have invited a rework, REPLY
     when you asked a question, NONE after LEG_DONE.
- Header comment: cite 22-CONTEXT.md, 22-COACH-LAYER-DECISION.md, the hard
  containment rule, and that the prompt's instructions are ADVISORY — the
  mechanical gates live in `tips.ts` (22-05) and `coach.ts` (22-10).
