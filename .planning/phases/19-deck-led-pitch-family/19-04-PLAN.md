<objective>
Ship the first two new deck modes as TYPE records plus prompts and nothing else:
the funding request (a budget or grant reviewer, an amount and a use of funds, no
equity anywhere) and the product pitch (a prospective customer who objects).

its own rubric and its own outcome shape, sharing Phase 14's deck capability
rather than copying it (REQ-88). REQ-89 is satisfied structurally: these records
never mention an ask price, an offered equity or a fair-value band, so there is
nothing to disable or hide.

Read `lib/pitch/deck-type.ts` and `lib/pitch/deck-prompts.ts` first — these
records are PEERS of that one, field-for-field, differing only where the mode
differs.

Output: four new files under `lib/pitch/`, two new `ENGINE_TYPES` entries, one
verify script.
