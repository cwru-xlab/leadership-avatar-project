```ts
type DeliveryCardState =
  | "scored"
  | "not_yet_measured"
  | "camera_off"
  | "typed_only"
  | "insufficient_data"
  | "not_scored";
```

Resolution order, exactly:
1. `metrics == null || metrics.cameraMode == null` -> `"not_yet_measured"`.
   No pipeline existed for this row. This is the ONLY path to today's copy, and
   it is keyed on the stored `cameraMode` being null rather than on the score
   being null — which is what separates a legacy row from a modern camera-off
   row (REQ-45/REQ-48).
2. `unscoredReason === "CAMERA_OFF_OPTOUT"` -> `"camera_off"`.
3. `unscoredReason === "TYPED_ONLY"` -> `"typed_only"`. It must NOT collapse
   into `"camera_off"`: typing is a modality choice, not a camera opt-out, and
   CONTEXT.md is explicit that the two are deliberately not symmetric.
4. `unscoredReason === "INSUFFICIENT_DATA"` -> `"insufficient_data"`.
5. `score === null` with no reason -> `"not_scored"` (evaluation ran and
   produced nothing — the existing 06-08 state, now reachable for Visual/Vocal
   too).
6. Otherwise `"scored"`.

Copy per state — reuse the existing muted card treatment
(`border-[#e3ebee] bg-[#f5f8fa]`, `text-[#8298a3]`) for every unscored state so
they read as one family, with distinct headline and sub-line:
- `not_yet_measured`: "Not yet measured" / "Requires video and audio analysis"
  — BYTE-IDENTICAL to today's strings (`:86-87`), so a legacy report is visually
  unchanged.
- `camera_off`: "Not measured" / "You practised with your camera off, so this
  category wasn't scored." Honest and non-judgemental — it was a legitimate
  choice.
- `typed_only`: "Not measured" / "You typed your answers, so there was no
  speech to measure." Explicitly not a penalty.
- `insufficient_data`: "Insufficient data" / "We couldn't measure this
  reliably — your camera or microphone stopped part-way through." States a
  technical cause, never implies the student performed badly.
- `not_scored`: "Not scored" / "The transcript didn't support a score." —
  reuse `ScoredCard`'s existing strings verbatim (`:115-116`).

Import the predicate from `lib/metrics/coverage.ts`; do not reimplement the
threshold here, and do NOT let it alter the displayed score in any way. Add a
comment that disclosure and scoring are deliberately two separate concerns and
that a coverage figure must never become a hidden second score.

Card height: the scored state now has more content, so replace the fixed
